# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in commitnote.

Bug reports, ideas and questions are welcome: open an
[issue](https://github.com/marcinjahn/commitnote/issues). For a bug, say which app you use (web,
Linux, iOS or Android), its version, and what you did, expected and saw instead. Never include note
contents, your passphrase or access tokens.

Pull requests aren't accepted for now, so please don't open one; an issue is the best way to
suggest a fix or a feature.
